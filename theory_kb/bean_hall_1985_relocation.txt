Title: Extensions of the Modified Due Date Rule
Authors: J. C. Bean and N. G. Hall (1985)
Source: Naval Research Logistics Quarterly, 32(3), 503-506.

Key Insights for Heuristic Design:
1. Dynamic dispatching rules schedule jobs one-by-one looking only at current time t. Because later decisions affect completion times of all subsequent jobs, early choices may become sub-optimal retrospectively.
2. In Augmented MDD, after each job j* is appended to the schedule, a backward relocation evaluation is performed:
   Each previously scheduled job in S \ {j*} is tested for relocation to the tail of S.
3. If moving an earlier job to the current tail reduces prefix tardiness, the relocation is accepted.
4. Relocations mitigate the myopic bias of greedy forward construction without incurring exponential computational cost.
